Zero-day activity dominated the week, led by confirmed pre-disclosure exploitation of two **SonicWall SMA 1000** vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-15409 and CVE-2026-15410). Sceptive://bl0ck confirms attackers had been chaining the flaws for weeks before the patch, gaining root access on VPN appliances and installing custom persistent malware. **Check Point** disclosed a critical authentication bypass (CVE-2026-16232) in Security Management and Multi-Domain Security Management that is being exploited to take over firewall management planes. **Microsoft SharePoint** servers are under active attack via CVE-2026-50522, a critical RCE where exploitation began within hours of proof-of-concept release; attackers are extracting IIS machine keys to establish long-term access that survives patching. The Russian state-linked group Laundry Bear continued exploiting unpatched **Zimbra** Collaboration servers (CVE-2025-66376) for email theft against government and commercial targets.

AI-driven automation was the defining attack pattern of the week. **Hugging Face** disclosed a breach of internal datasets and service credentials attributed to an autonomous AI agent system, one of the first publicly confirmed end-to-end intrusions executed by agentic tooling. The JadePuffer group deployed ENCFORGE, an agentic ransomware strain automating database discovery and extortion against AI infrastructure. Researchers also documented AI coding agents being manipulated into recommending more than 7,600 malicious GitHub repositories to developers. On the mass exploitation front, our honeypot sensors registered tens of thousands of attempts against the **WordPress** "wp2shell" chain (CVE-2026-63030 and CVE-2026-60137), an unauthenticated RCE affecting WordPress core 6.8 and later that requires no plugins; notably, the underlying flaws were discovered with the help of a frontier AI model, and exploitation was underway within hours of public exploit code. **erviceNow AI Platform** instances are being hit through CVE-2026-6875, a pre-auth sandbox escape to RCE, with in-the-wild exploitation observed just days after disclosure across multiple distinct attack routes.

Products with critical, actively exploited vulnerabilities this week: SonicWall SMA 1000 (CVE-2026-15409, CVE-2026-15410), ServiceNow AI Platform (CVE-2026-6875), Microsoft SharePoint (CVE-2026-50522), Check Point Security Management and Multi-Domain Security Management (CVE-2026-16232), WordPress core 6.8+ (CVE-2026-63030, CVE-2026-60137), and Zimbra Collaboration Suite (CVE-2025-66376). As CTO or CISO, treat internet-facing SonicWall SMA and SharePoint instances as potentially compromised rather than merely vulnerable: patching alone is insufficient where credential theft or machine key extraction has occurred, so rotate secrets, invalidate keys, and hunt for webshells and unauthorized persistence. Take ServiceNow and Check Point management interfaces off the public internet and restrict them to trusted networks. Verify WordPress estates are at fixed versions, since exposure requires no plugins or authentication. Finally, inventory where autonomous AI agents and coding assistants operate inside your environment and constrain their credentials and repository sources, as this week demonstrated they are now both an attack surface and an attack vector.
