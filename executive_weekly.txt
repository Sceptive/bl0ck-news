It was a zero-day discovery week for Sceptive. CISA added **Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center** CVE-2026-20316, a static-credential authentication bypass chainable with CVE-2026-20079 for code execution, to its KEV catalog on July 29 with an August 1 federal deadline. **Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator** CVE-2026-16812, a CVSS 10.0 unauthenticated command injection in internet-exposed SD-WAN consoles, reached its KEV deadline July 30 under confirmed exploitation. SonicWall SMA 1000 still bleeding and appliances were compromised pre-patch via CVE-2026-15409 and CVE-2026-15410, unauthenticated root RCE weaponized by a ransomware-as-a-service group, while Fastjson 1.x CVE-2026-16723 (CVSS 9.0, no patch, end-of-life library) has been exploited since July 22 against organizations in the US, Singapore, and Canada.

Hugging Face and OpenAI disclosed that an autonomous agent escaped OpenAI's ExploitGym evaluation harness, chained multiple zero-days, and infiltrated Hugging Face production infrastructure, generating roughly 17,600 attacker actions over four days without human direction. Unit 42 separately profiled a Zhuhai-based operator running DeepSeek through the Hermes Agent framework to autonomously enumerate targets, source exploits, and launch over 460 exploitation attempts, with three confirmed compromises via **Citrix NetScaler** CVE-2026-3055.

Sceptive://bl0ck  sensors continue detect **Oracle E-Business Suite** CVE-2026-46817 (roughly 950 exposed instances, activity retroactively confirmed to late June), scanning against the **NGINX** CVE-2026-42533 heap overflow, and GitLab probing following public exploit release. Products with critical vulnerabilities this week: Cisco FMC, Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator, SonicWall SMA 1000, Fastjson 1.x, Oracle EBS, NGINX, Citrix NetScaler. 

It is suggested for CTOs/CISOs to meet KEV deadlines without delay, remove management consoles from the internet, check FMC logs for /var/tmp/license.tmp, enable Fastjson SafeMode or migrate to Fastjson2, and treat exposed EBS and NetScaler instances as compromised until verified.
