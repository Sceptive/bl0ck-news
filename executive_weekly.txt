Zero-day and post-disclosure exploitation dominated the week. **CISA** added four actively exploited flaws to the KEV catalog on 18 August, issuing Binding Operational Directive 26-04 with a 21 August deadline: **VMware vCenter** Syslog directory traversal **CVE-2026-59310** (CVSS 9.8), **Apple macOS** Screen Sharing **CVE-2026-65400**, **Microsoft SharePoint** **CVE-2026-55040**, and **Windows** IKE Service **CVE-2026-33824**. A China-nexus APT tracked by **QUIRSO** compromised 361 organisations across 47 countries within five days of Broadcom's vCenter patch, dropping cron persistence and reverse_ssh, with victim concentrations in Germany, the US, Türkiye, Iran and France. **GitLab** **CVE-2026-19478** (CVSS 9.4), a GraphQL directive code injection allowing unauthenticated repository deletion, saw exploitation within days of disclosure, confirmed against **watchTowr** honeypot infrastructure. **Metabase** **CVE-2026-72898** (CVSS 10.0) remained under exploitation, having already hit **Framework**, **n8n** and **Kilo Code**.

Automation reached new scale. A Zhuhai-based actor operating as knaithe/KnYuan embedded **DeepSeek** into the **Hermes Agent** framework, autonomously scanning over 647,000 internet-facing targets and exploiting them without per-step human input. The **ZOOMSDAY** zero-click chain against **Zoom** annotation was reportedly weaponised in under 24 hours using AI models. Mass exploitation of the **WordPress** wp2shell chain (**CVE-2026-63030**, **CVE-2026-60137**) generated over 45 million attempts from roughly 150,000 unique sources, indiscriminate enough to strike Drupal hosts. Supply chain activity included 14 trojanised **npm** packages carrying RedC2 4.0 and Sapphire Sleet typosquats in **Rust** crates arrayref, internment and append-only-vec.

Critical exposure sits with **VMware vCenter**, **GitLab** self-hosted 18.2–19.2, **Metabase**, **Microsoft SharePoint**, **WordPress** core, **Apple macOS**, **Cisco ASA/FTD** and **Zoom**. Treat the patch window as under 72 hours, audit vCenter for rogue cron entries and reverse_ssh, review GitLab logs for `@gl_introduced`, restrict `/api/graphql`, and rotate database credentials stored in Metabase.
