Edge appliances carried the zero-day load this week. **Cisco** published an advisory on 28 October for **CVE-2026-76418** (CVSS 8.6), an unauthenticated flaw in Secure Firewall **ASA** and **FTD** SSL VPN exploited to force reloads and knock remote-access VPNs offline; **CISA** added it to KEV on 29 October. **Ivanti** patched **CVE-2026-111357** and **CVE-2026-118527** on 29 October, closing the Connect Secure root chain that **Volexity** attributes to **UTA0576** and that opened with **CVE-2026-104935**; post-exploitation included log wiping, credential theft, and Kerberoasting. **GitLab** **CVE-2026-97229** (CVSS 10.0), an integer overflow in Ruby's Onigmo regex engine reachable through CI/CD configuration, entered KEV the same day after **Alpine Linux** reported repeated attacks against its instance.

AI-driven offense and mass exploitation converged. **Microsoft** linked China-aligned **Storm-0940** to the **ConnectWise** supply chain compromise, run through the open-source agentic framework **ARTEX**, with sign-in attempts against more than 2,250 tenants and privileged cloud access reached in under three hours. **Volexity** assesses the Ivanti chain itself was developed with LLM assistance. **NorForge** detailed Cairn-orchestrated agents hitting over 1,200 sites at roughly 50 dollars per successful breach. On the mass-exploitation side, PHP2Shell (**CVE-2026-109618**, **CVE-2026-109619**, CVSS 9.8) was hit on **AWS** MadPot honeypots four days before disclosure, **Sansec** reported hundreds of **Adobe Commerce** stores breached via **CVE-2026-48356** (CVSS 9.3), and **ELLIO** caught the first Node2Shell exploit traffic for **Google Brotli** **CVE-2026-111497** from an IP also scanning for PHP2Shell.

Critical flaws this week sit in **Cisco** ASA and FTD, **Ivanti** Connect Secure, **GitLab** CE and EE, **PHP**, **Adobe Commerce** and **Magento**, **Google Brotli** as embedded in **Node.js**, and **ConnectWise** cloud apps. For a CTO or CISO, the tiers to verify are VPN and firewall concentrators, self-hosted source control, and any service decompressing untrusted Brotli, since adversaries now move from disclosure to exploitation in hours and to tenant-wide credential abuse in minutes.
