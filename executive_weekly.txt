Zero-day activity concentrated on edge and collaboration infrastructure. **Citrix** **NetScaler ADC/Gateway** **CVE-2026-8452**, originally published as denial-of-service only, was reclassified after unauthenticated RCE was confirmed in the wild; CISA added it to KEV on 26 August with a 29 August federal deadline, and defenders observed web shells named x.php and z.php dropped on compromised appliances. **PaperCut NG/MF** was hit by pre-authentication RCE **CVE-2026-81578** and unsafe reflection flaw **CVE-2026-82078**, added to KEV on 27 August after Huntress documented intrusions against version 24 servers before patches shipped. **TrueConf Server** **CVE-2026-72529** and **CVE-2026-72530** were chained by Head Mare to deploy PhantomCore against Eastern European government and industrial targets, while **Zimbra Collaboration** **CVE-2026-73570** command injection was tied to roughly 274 compromised servers.

AI-driven operations moved from research to production. Five US agencies issued advisory **AA26-231A** on AI-generated exploit scripts probing **Siemens S7** PLCs across water, energy, and manufacturing, with attackers achieving read and write access to PLC memory and ladder logic. CISA also flagged **Ajax.NET Professional** **CVE-2021-23758** as part of AI-scaled automated exploitation. Mass exploitation followed the same pattern: **VMware vCenter** **CVE-2026-59310** produced 361 observed victim IPs and Babuk-derived ransomware deployment, and **GitLab** **CVE-2026-19478** saw self-hosted instances attacked within minutes of patch release.

Critical exposure this week sits in **Citrix NetScaler**, **PaperCut NG/MF**, **TrueConf Server**, **Zimbra**, **VMware vCenter**, **GitLab**, **Next.js** (**CVE-2026-75604**), **Microsoft Entra ID** (**CVE-2026-69836**), and **Siemens S7** controllers. Cautions for CTOs and CISOs: vendor severity ratings are being revised upward post-release, roughly 47% of PaperCut estates remain on unfixed versions, and OT segmentation gaps are now being probed by automated tooling rather than manual operators.
