Edge infrastructure carried the week's zero-days. **F5** disclosed **CVE-2026-94127** (CVSS 9.8), an unauthenticated heap overflow giving remote code execution on **BIG-IP APM** instances acting as an OAuth Authorization Server, with exploitation confirmed at disclosure. **Check Point** issued emergency fixes for **CVE-2026-93616** (9.8), a pre-auth path traversal on Security Management Servers exploited since at least 23 July, and confirmed attacks on Spark firewalls via **CVE-2026-85102** (9.8) since 12 September. **Arista** VeloCloud Orchestrator **CVE-2026-93952** (10.0) completed the four entries **CISA** added to KEV on 22 September with a three-day deadline. Later additions covered **WSO2** **CVE-2026-5430** (10.0), **Adobe Commerce** **CVE-2026-71362** (9.1), **Microsoft SharePoint** **CVE-2026-65660**, **MikroTik** RouterOS **CVE-2026-67279**, and **Citrix NetScaler** **CVE-2026-88771** and **CVE-2026-88772**.

AI-driven operations reached retail at scale. **Gambit Security** documented a single operator running the open-source Strix, Cairn, and Hermes agent stack on **Claude** Opus 4.6 via OpenRouter, compromising more than 100 e-commerce sites, breaching 27 organizations in five days, and stealing over 600,000 payment cards at roughly 25 dollars of model spend per target. **WordPress** core **CVE-2026-87902** (9.2) saw its first exploitation attempt at 11:49 UTC on 22 September, the day the patch shipped, with traffic up tenfold by 23 September and about 350,000 exposed installs unpatched per **Shadowserver**. **Mandiant** reported **ShinyHunters** renewing its **Oracle PeopleSoft** **CVE-2026-35273** campaign with a `/%50SEMHUB/` WAF bypass, and **GreyNoise** counted 996 **Zyxel** GS1900 switches compromised via **CVE-2026-7273** across 48 countries.

Critical flaws this week hit **F5 BIG-IP APM**, **Check Point** Security Management and Spark, **Arista** VeloCloud Orchestrator, **WSO2**, **Adobe Commerce**, **Citrix NetScaler**, **WordPress** core, **Oracle PeopleSoft**, and **Zyxel** GS1900. For a CTO or CISO, the exposure worth confirming is the management plane of network and security appliances, OAuth and VPN termination points, and public commerce stacks, since attackers held working exploits before or within hours of every fix above.
