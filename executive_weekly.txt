Pre-disclosure exploitation defined the week. **PHP** **CVE-2026-109618** and **CVE-2026-109619** (PHP2Shell, both CVSS 9.2) give unauthenticated remote code execution through crafted HTTP requests. We have logged a working attempt on 17 October, four days before the 21 October advisory, and **Cloudflare** counted tens of thousands of attempts within 45 minutes of disclosure and millions by 22 October, many pushing the Potassium **Mirai** fork. **Arista** patched **CVE-2026-114625**, a VeloCloud Orchestrator SQL injection exploited as a zero-day. The **Plex** Media Server chain **CVE-2026-96652**, **CVE-2026-96654**, **CVE-2026-96655**, and **CVE-2026-96656** was weaponized one day after the 16 October proof of concept, dropping XMRig miners and proxy malware. **CISA** also added **Collabora Online** **CVE-2026-110447**, **Siemens** Software Center **CVE-2026-110936**, and **SiteGuard** **CVE-2026-106931** to the KEV catalog.

Agentic tooling is now in nation-state hands. **Microsoft** attributed the **ConnectWise** cloud backup supply chain compromise to China-aligned **Storm-0940**, which used the multi-agent framework ARTEX to automate password spraying and OAuth abuse, attempting sign-ins to more than 2,250 tenants and gaining privileged cloud access in under three hours at several victims. **Gambit Security** documented a five-day campaign in which agentic frameworks Hermes, Strix, and Cairn hit 105 e-commerce sites, compromised 27, and stole at least 600,000 payment card records at an average cost of $25.46 per target. **Symantec** trapped an autonomous attack agent that hijacks unauthenticated OpenAI-compatible endpoints as its planning engine.

Critical exposure this week sits in **PHP** 8.2 through 8.5 and all unsupported branches, **Arista** VeloCloud Orchestrator, **Plex** Media Server, **Atlassian** Data Center products (**CVE-2026-21589**), **Collabora Online**, **Siemens** Software Center, and **Microsoft** Windows via the Mark of the Web bypass **CVE-2026-96951**. For a CTO or CISO, confirm whether any internet-facing PHP runtime remains on an unfixed branch, whether MSP tooling such as ConnectWise holds standing OAuth grants into your tenant, and whether any internal LLM endpoint accepts unauthenticated requests.
