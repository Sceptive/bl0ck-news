
Edge appliances still carries zero-day load. **Ivanti** patched two more Connect Secure flaws, **CVE-2026-111357** and **CVE-2026-118527**, chained in the wild with **CVE-2026-104935** for unauthenticated root; **Volexity** attributes the activity to UTA0576, which pulled Windows LAPS passwords and BitLocker keys from Active Directory. **SonicWall** confirmed exploitation of **CVE-2026-19868**, a SAML enforcement bypass that lets SSLVPN users skip MFA, added to **CISA** KEV on 6 November with ransomware use flagged. **Binarly** showed that **Zyxel** GS1900 Patch 2 fails to stop **CVE-2026-107441**, the unauthenticated root exploit behind the Red Heron intrusions on 996 switches. **OpenSSH** **CVE-2026-123158** ("SSHole", CVSS 8.1), an oversized-password bypass on musl systems such as Alpine, gave root on a **Previdian** honeypot under nine hours after publication.

Mass exploitation centred on **Brotli** **CVE-2026-111497** ("Node2Shell"), a decoder out-of-bounds write inherited by **Node.js**, **Bun**, **Envoy**, **Istio**, **Palo Alto Networks** PAN-OS and **VMware** SDDC Manager, with **Previdian** and **ELLIO** honeypots logging live attempts ahead of the 10 November federal deadline. **Microsoft** documented actors driving the ARTEX agentic framework from GitHub secret search to **Azure** Key Vault exfiltration in five minutes, with service principal sign-ins across dozens of applications in multiple tenants. **CrowdStrike** detailed four agentic campaigns, including a Russian-speaking group that accessed 726 tenant databases in one operation and an agent that harvested domain controller credentials from Argentine hospitals in roughly 20 minutes.

Critical exposure sits in **Ivanti** Connect Secure, **SonicWall** SonicOS Gen7 and Gen8, **Zyxel** GS1900, **OpenSSH** on Alpine and musl, and every product bundling **Brotli**, notably **Node.js**, **PAN-OS** and **VMware** Cloud Foundation. For a CTO or CISO, the cautions are to treat VPN and firewall appliances as already probed, verify that SAML and MFA are enforced rather than merely configured, and audit service principals and leaked repository secrets, since agentic tooling now turns one exposed key into tenant-wide access in minutes.
