Three actively exploited zero-days defined the week. **Metabase** **CVE-2026-72898**, a CVSS 10.0 unauthenticated SQL injection in the password reset endpoint, granted full administrator access and reached the CISA KEV catalog on 11 August, with roughly 2,500 instances internet-exposed and **Framework**, **n8n** and **Kilo Code** confirming customer data exposure. **Cisco** disclosed **CVE-2026-20349** (CVSS 8.6) on 12 August, exploited since early August to crash **Secure Firewall ASA** and **FTD** remote access VPN appliances with no workaround available. **Microsoft** Patch Tuesday on 11 August covered 421 CVEs, including **CVE-2026-68820**, an exploited afd.sys WinSock use-after-free giving SYSTEM privileges.

AI-assisted offense moved from theory to demonstrated capability. Researchers chained **CVE-2026-53413**, **CVE-2026-53414** and **CVE-2026-53415** into "ZOOMSDAY", a zero-click RCE against **Zoom** client 7.0.5 across Windows, macOS, iOS and Android, built with fewer than 20 prompts to public AI models in under 24 hours. The RovoBlast technique against **Atlassian Rovo** injected malicious prompts into authenticated sessions to pull enterprise data from connected services. On mass exploitation, our sensor network and Defused telemetry recorded automated scanning against **SAP Commerce Cloud** **CVE-2026-58231** (CVSS 10.0) within three days of its 12 August patch, with no public proof of concept in circulation. **Progress Kemp LoadMaster** **CVE-2026-8037** (CVSS 9.6) entered KEV after 792 attempts from 65 addresses in 18 countries, and **N-able N-central** **CVE-2026-18577** remained under exploitation following an incomplete fix.

Critical exposure concentrates in **Metabase**, **SAP Commerce Cloud**, **Cisco ASA/FTD**, **Progress Kemp LoadMaster**, **N-able N-central** and **Zoom**. CTOs and CISOs should treat edge VPN, RMM and BI platforms as the current breach path, assume patch-to-exploit windows under 72 hours, and validate that vendor fixes are complete rather than partial.
