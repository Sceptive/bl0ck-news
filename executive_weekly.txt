**Citrix NetScaler** ADC and Gateway zero-days **CVE-2026-88771** (CVSS 9.8) and **CVE-2026-88772** (8.1) were confirmed by **Mandiant** and **Google** Threat Intelligence Group as exploited since early September by suspected state-sponsored actors deploying the **SLAPSHOT** tunneling daemon and **WHIPSHOT** web shell. After **watchTowr** published a proof of concept on 29 September, honeypot sensors logged live exploitation within minutes against roughly 42,000 exposed instances, fewer than 10 percent patched. **Cisco** Catalyst SD-WAN Manager **CVE-2026-76504**, an authentication bypass granting admin API access, and **Fortinet FortiMail** **CVE-2026-104286** (CVSS 9.8), an unauthenticated path traversal enabling arbitrary file writes, both entered the **CISA** KEV catalog on 1 October. **Apple** patched CoreGraphics **CVE-2026-86950**, reported by **Meta**, after targeted zero-click exploitation. **WordPress** core **CVE-2026-87902** (CVSS 9.2) was probed the same day the fix shipped and carried functional webshells within 48 hours, with counting over 350,000 unpatched sites.

AI-assisted offense shortened every window above. The **Microsoft** Digital Defense Report released 1 October puts the median time from disclosure to active weaponization under 24 hours against a 30 to 60 day enterprise remediation cycle, and documents **JADEPUFFER** autonomously selecting targets, delivering ransom demands, and running extortion with minimal human input. **Anthropic** reported on 29 September that the open-weight **Zhipu GLM-5.3** built working end-to-end exploits in 50 of 410 ExploitBench attempts, near frontier parity, and that weight abliteration lifted attack engagement from 0 to 100 percent; NIST's CAISI called it the most cyber-capable open-weight model yet.

Critical flaws this week affect **Citrix NetScaler**, **Cisco** Catalyst SD-WAN Manager, **Fortinet FortiMail**, **Apple** iOS and macOS, **WordPress** core, and **Zammad** (**CVE-2026-102489**, **CVE-2026-102490**). For a CTO or CISO, patching NetScaler does not remove installed backdoors, so compromise assessment and credential rotation must accompany the upgrade, and every internet-exposed remote-access, mail, or management plane should be treated as already scanned.
